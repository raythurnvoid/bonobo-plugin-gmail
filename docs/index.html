Bonobo Gmail

A Gmail backup plugin for Press. It copies sent and received emails into ordinary Markdown files in your selected workspace. Attachments are saved beside each email.

The plugin requests only read-only Gmail access. It cannot send mail, delete mail, or change your mailbox. You connect through Google consent and finish on your original Press page.

Your workspace, your files

Saved mail follows Press Files permissions. People with access to those files can read them. You can search the files and ask a Press agent to read them.

Disconnect stops future sync and clears the plugin's saved Gmail access. Existing files stay until you remove them in Press. Disconnect before uninstalling.

Development status

This integration is being tested. Public release, Google verification, and hosting capacity checks remain open.
